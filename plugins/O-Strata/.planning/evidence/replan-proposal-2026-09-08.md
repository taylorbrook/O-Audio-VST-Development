# O-Strata re-plan proposal — 2026-09-08 (uncommitted; awaiting Taylor's go)

**Decision so far (Taylor, after the listening pass):** the baked geometry tables sound good but do not warrant a separate plugin → they ship as an O-Prism factory bank (`plugins/O-Prism/.planning/improvements/geometry-wavetables.md`). O-Strata pulls the **live terrain oscillator** forward and demotes mesh slicing.

## New O-Strata v1.0 in one paragraph

O-Prism's voice, filters, modulation, FX and tuning engine with a **live wave-terrain oscillator** as the oscillator type: a closed orbit scans an analytic or PNG terrain at audio rate, with orbit size / rotation / centre and terrain spatial frequency as **audio-rate mod-matrix destinations**, trajectory feedback, per-oscillator 2× oversampling (4× HQ) and the verified **Chebyshev bandlimited mode** at 1× (research §7.2). The 3D view (§7.3) shows terrain + orbit + a 30 Hz playhead and is draggable. This is the part of the research with sonic identity; nothing in the O-Prism bank duplicates it. Baked geometry (mesh slicing, SDF/fBm volume orbits) moves to **v1.1** as a secondary "Baked" source type, or stays out — the O-Prism bank covers that sound.

## What carries over from the current O-Strata

- The fork, full `Strata` rename, wavetable-library strip, `JUCE_WEB_BROWSER` editor guard, `juce_cryptography` link, UI gate fixtures, the headless smoke harness pattern, CHANGELOG/NOTES/PLUGINS.md plumbing (all of Stage 1's mechanics).

- The symmetry finding: **default orbits must be off-centre** (centred orbit over an even terrain doubles the pitch) — becomes a requirement and a harness gate.

An hour with it answers "does audio-rate orbit modulation and trajectory feedback sound alive to me?" for free — the same test the baked tables just went through. Do not copy its code (GPL-3.0; clean-room formulas as already decided).

Entry: reset STATUS to stage 1 and run `/plugin-discuss O-Strata 1-foundation` (the workflow has no "re-enter a verified stage" command; STATUS is the switch).
6. **Stage 2** — live oscillator DSP with a render harness whose first gates are §7.2's: non-harmonic energy at C6 ≤ −60 dB at 2× (≤ −90 dB in Chebyshev mode), CPU at 16 voices, no allocation, pitch correct on every default (the symmetry gate).
7. Stages 3–4 as the new ROADMAP defines them (3D view, import, persistence, presets, CI, listening pass).

## Alternative kept in view

Keep the baked pipeline in v1.0 as a secondary source beside the live oscillator ("demote, don't remove"). Cost: the whole current Stage 2 (five phases) on top of the live oscillator; the plugin becomes the largest in the catalogue by a wide margin, and v1.0 still overlaps the O-Prism bank. Recommendation: v1.1.
