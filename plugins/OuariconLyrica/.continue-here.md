# Resume Point

## Current State: Phase 2.9 Complete - Glissando Controller Implemented

Phase 2.9 (Glissando Controller) is complete. OuariconLyrica now has a sophisticated glissando system supporting three modes: Off (normal discrete notes), Free (continuous pitch sweep using juce::SmoothedValue), and Scale-Locked (discrete steps through scale degrees). The GlissandoController integrates with the TuningEngine to retrieve scale frequencies for scale-locked mode. Free mode provides smooth, portamento-like sweeps between notes, while scale-locked mode steps through chromatic scale degrees at a configurable speed (default 10 notes/second). The glissando controller updates frequency per-sample in the audio render thread for smooth, artifact-free pitch transitions.

## Key Architecture Decisions

- **Core Algorithm:** Bidirectional Digital Waveguide Synthesis
- **Polyphony Target:** 16-32 voices at <50% CPU
- **String Model:** Dual delay lines with bridge/nut filters + stiffness allpass
- **Sympathetic Resonance:** Frequency-domain coupling with material-based intensity
- **Body Resonance:** 50-100ms convolution IR (with modal fallback)
- **Tuning:** MTS-ESP + Scala + 12-TET fallback
- **Glissando:** Free (continuous) and Scale-Locked (stepped) modes
