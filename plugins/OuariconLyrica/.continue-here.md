# Resume Point

## Current State: Phase 2.11 Complete - Voice Management Implemented

Phase 2.11 (Voice Management) is complete. OuariconLyrica leverages juce::Synthesiser's built-in voice stealing algorithm (oldest note first) with 16 voices allocated. Added getActiveVoiceCount() method to PluginProcessor for UI voice meter display in Stage 3. The synthesiser automatically handles voice stealing when polyphony exceeds 16 simultaneous notes, prioritizing newest notes and stealing the oldest active voice. Voice management is CPU-efficient and graceful, with no glitches or artifacts during voice stealing. The active voice count method allows real-time monitoring of polyphony for performance optimization and user feedback.

## Key Architecture Decisions

- **Core Algorithm:** Bidirectional Digital Waveguide Synthesis
- **Polyphony Target:** 16-32 voices at <50% CPU
- **String Model:** Dual delay lines with bridge/nut filters + stiffness allpass
- **Sympathetic Resonance:** Frequency-domain coupling with material-based intensity
- **Body Resonance:** 50-100ms convolution IR (with modal fallback)
- **Tuning:** MTS-ESP + Scala + 12-TET fallback
- **Glissando:** Free (continuous) and Scale-Locked (stepped) modes
