# Resume Point

## Current State: Phase 2.6 Complete - Body Resonance Implemented

Phase 2.6 (Body Resonance) is complete. OuariconLyrica now has a modal synthesis body resonance system using 5 resonant bandpass filters (300, 400, 600, 900, 1200 Hz) to simulate harp soundboard vibrations. The bodySize parameter scales modal frequencies, woodType selects Q and amplitude profiles (Spruce, Maple, Exotic, Synthetic), and bodyResonance controls wet/dry mix. This adds acoustic depth and warmth to the physical modeling engine.

## Key Architecture Decisions

- **Core Algorithm:** Bidirectional Digital Waveguide Synthesis
- **Polyphony Target:** 16-32 voices at <50% CPU
- **String Model:** Dual delay lines with bridge/nut filters + stiffness allpass
- **Sympathetic Resonance:** Frequency-domain coupling with material-based intensity
- **Body Resonance:** 50-100ms convolution IR (with modal fallback)
- **Tuning:** MTS-ESP + Scala + 12-TET fallback
- **Glissando:** Free (continuous) and Scale-Locked (stepped) modes
