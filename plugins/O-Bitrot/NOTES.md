- **2026-08-18 (v1.10.0):** **The Rot family** (improvement brief item 8) — five new params, a seventh clocked family, and **bit-identical to v1.9.0 for every existing session and preset**. Research §3.5 lists four corrupt-file mechanisms and the engine shipped exactly one (buffer shuffling, via the read head); a grep for `xor`/`bitflip`/`sticky`/`byte` across `Source/dsp` returned nothing while BRIEF.md:12 claimed "the full physical-to-digital spectrum of broken playback". `RotStage` adds the other three: **Flip** (a rate-limited window XORing individual samples in the 16-bit domain, then clipping — one flipped bit is one impulse), **Stick** (the decoder hangs, one sample held 10–80 ms — a DC plateau), and **Garble** (a 30–300 ms wrong-decode stretch emitting noise *at the programme's own RMS*, which is the entire artifact: fixed-level noise reads as a broken plugin, level-tracking noise reads as a corrupt file). **Rot is the first OVERLAY-ONLY family** in the v1.9.0 vocabulary — it changes no head position and no rate, so it never enters the ownership contest and never touches the `arbitration` stream, which is what made adding a seventh family a pure append instead of a rewrite of the contest. **Containment is the release's load-bearing property**: the gate short-circuits *before* its RNG draw while `ROT_ENABLE` is off, so the family costs nothing and perturbs no stream until switched on — asserted with no tolerance by `R5` and confirmed by every cross-version anchor (A3×3, V1, N7, N8) still matching its recorded digest untouched. **One new `RngBank::rot` stream serves the whole family**, drawn at BOTH tick instants and per sample — the shape `scratch` was split out to avoid. It is safe because rot is a *single subsystem* whose draws all land inside one per-sample loop iteration in fixed order, making the sequence a pure function of the absolute sample index; `R4` measures that at {512}, {4096} and ragged rather than trusting the argument. **The instructive failure: the post-clip was unreachable in the first cut.** Bit 15 (the sign) had been excluded on the reasoning that polarity inversion is a different artifact from an impulse — but a single flip of bit ≤ 14 on a word already inside ±32767 can never leave ±32768, so the brief's "post-clip" had nothing to do, and `R1` **passed with the clip deleted**. The sabotage run is what caught it; both the implementation (bit 15 now in range, XOR reaches ±2.0 FS) and the probe (DEPTH 100, where the field reaches the sign bit) were corrected, and deleting the clip now takes the measured peak to 1.5000. - **2026-08-18 (v1.9.0):** Overlay-class arbitration (improvement brief item 6) — no new params, no state change, and **the render moves only on ticks where two or more families fire**. Until now exactly one family could act per tick: every win called `release()` on the other two, and a tape+vinyl double-fire dropped one event outright. Right for a transport, wrong for everything else — research §4.2's RSBrokenMedia rolls its categories independently, and the engine already half-admitted it, since `CDSkip::release` only ever touched `Loop`, so a bounded conceal/mute rung could *finish* under a tape win but never *start* under one. Events are now **OWNER** (tape stop/bend, CD loop, vinyl jump/locked groove — rate or head position, so still single-winner via the `arbitration` stream, because there is one read head) or **OVERLAY** (CD conceal, CD mute+tick, tape dropout, vinyl standalone pop — gain/filter/artifact domain, orthogonal to position and rate), and overlays fire **whenever their family's roll succeeds regardless of who owns the tick**. This is what makes "Total Media Failure" sound like everything failing at once rather than like a queue. **The standalone vinyl pop is the one new event**: a vinyl roll that succeeded then lost used to produce *nothing*, which is not what the physics says happened — the stylus met the debris either way and only the groove skip is in question — so a lost roll now fires the pop alone while the transport stays where the winner put it, inheriting the v1.7.0 tick/pop/scratch taxonomy for free. **The enabling refactor is moving the kind rolls out of the winner's branch**: a losing family never even decided what its event *was*, so `onWin` split into a pure `rollRung`/`rollKind` classification plus `applyLoop`/`applyOverlay`/`applyOwner`/`applyStandalonePop`. **Releases are class-aware** — a family yields its transport state when it does not own the tick *unless* it fired an overlay and nobody owns, which is not a new exception but the general form of v1.8.0 already leaving a bend ramping under a tape-dropout win. **The trap: a CD overlay must leave a running loop through `recoveryJump`, never `release()`** — `endLoop` can enter the v1.6.0 servo seek and 100–400 ms of silence would swallow the very rung being installed; the old code had this right for a rung change and it had to survive the split, so Arbitration does not call `cd.release()` on a family that fired an overlay. **Containment is proven, not asserted**: a tick with at most one firer consumes the same draws, makes the same release calls and installs the same event, so single-family renders are bit-identical — new probe **`A3`** pins three of them (tape with `TAPE_DROP 40` for the conditional draw, CD at 0.5 for all three rungs, vinyl for both kinds) against digests recorded by compiling that probe against the **v1.8.0** tree at git `627f8afb` **before the first v1.9.0 edit**. `V1` **re-anchored a third time** (`0x44a5de77d572facd` → `0x70e744c93cbcc2a3`) and this time NOT narrowed, because the move is the feature: its config collides on ~60% of ticks and at `CD_SEVERITY 0` the rung roll can only reach conceal, so CD is pure-overlay there and now lands on all of them; the old number is kept and asserted **negatively**, so a build where the split silently failed reads as a failure. `N7`/`N8` untouched — `configureCanonicalPostRender` disables all three transport families. Harness **102/102**, 3 probes added; **`A1` and `A2` were run against v1.8.0 first and fail there** (12/31 each) — without that they would be decoration. **`A1`'s own trap is worth keeping**: its first draft read 0/31 with a median HF ratio of **0.00000**, because `VINYL_PROB 100` walks the head's lag out to the ring budget within seconds and early in a render the material 8–9 s behind live is the ring's *pre-history*, i.e. zeros — the dip bar was being applied to silence; it now renders 20 s, scans from 12 s, and gates on the scan region's magnitude **and** a non-degenerate median so that failure cannot re-present as a pass. auval PASS, pluginval strictness-10 SUCCESS. Installed.

- **2026-08-17 (v1.8.0):** Codec chain depth (improvement brief items 16 + 29 + 7) — one new param, and **the codec family's render moves whenever `CODEC_ENABLE` is on** (it defaults off, so an untouched v1.7.0 session is bit-identical). All three items are the same complaint from three angles: `CodecStage` was a filter chain with a quantizer in it, not a telephone link — no dynamics, a mu-law *curve* rather than the standard's table, and it never lost a frame because the packet stage dropped PCM BEFORE the encoder. **`CODEC_AGC`** (default 100) inserts research §3.3's missing fast compressor after the mode blend and before `post1`: 1 ms attack, 50 ms release, −20 dBFS threshold, 4:1, fixed +10 dB makeup — measured +10.0 dB lift on a −26 dBFS tone against −0.45 dB on a −6 dBFS one, a 10.45 dB spread. It is a **depth, not a switch**: gain is assembled in dB and scaled there, so 0 is `exp(0)` = exactly `1.0f` and restores the v1.7.0 gain structure to the bit. **Mu-law is now the segmented G.711 codec** — ITU-T/Sun reference encoder (14-bit domain, bias 33, clip 8159) against a compile-time 256-entry decode LUT (16-bit bias 132); silence still encodes to `0xFF` → **exactly 0.0f**, and full scale now decodes to **32124/32768 = −0.17 dBFS**, which is where a real landline clips rather than at 1.0. **Codec-domain frame loss**: with GSM and PACKET both on, a lost frame re-feeds the decoder the previous frame's BYTES — the encoder still runs, because it sits at the far end of the link and loss happens in transit, and the decoder's own LTP history evolving on each repeat is where the metallic warble comes from (it is not a buffer being looped). −3 dB per repeat, ramped across the frame it applies to, hard mute at **16 consecutive frames — 320 ms, GSM 06.11's own mute point**. **The two 20 ms grids are coupled by LOOKUP, not by counting**: nominally both 960 samples at 48 kHz, but the 8 kHz latch is FRACTIONAL so the frame boundary rides its crossings and drifts against the packet grid; the processor hands `CodecStage` the loss flag every sample and the stage consumes whichever value lands on the sample that closes a frame — read BEFORE `PacketLossStage::processSample`, which advances the grid at its end and would otherwise hand back the NEXT packet's state. **The AGC envelope is primed, not zeroed** — a 1 ms attack meeting a cold envelope sits at full makeup, and the codec's first signal arrives ~20 ms in, past the enable fade, so it met the whole +10 dB and overshot to **+3.7 dBFS**; primed at its unity-gain level the residual is **+1.7 dB**, the ordinary transient fed by the structural-delay silence the envelope releases through. Re-entry after a PLC mute still overshoots and stays that way — it is the compressor working on a real 60 dB step. - **2026-08-17 (v1.5.0):** Media noise beds (improvement brief items 4 + 19) — five new params, every one default 0 and exactly transparent there, and **no render-affecting changes at all**. ## Latency Scheme (Stage 2)

Constant reported latency of 20 ms in ALL modes:
`setLatencySamples(ceil(0.020 * fs))` once in `prepareToPlay`. CodecStage
owns the alignment: a hand-rolled integer delay of exactly that figure when
the codec is disabled or in Mu-law mode; the GSM 160-frame chain replaces it
structurally in GSM mode (one 8 kHz frame = 0.020 s). `DryWetMixer::
setWetLatency` aligns the dry path. The first 20 ms of GSM output after
engagement is silence (output frame primed with zeros; the 10 ms
CODEC_ENABLE fade covers it). At non-integer `0.020*fs` host rates the GSM
path misaligns by < 1 sample versus the plain delay — accepted.
