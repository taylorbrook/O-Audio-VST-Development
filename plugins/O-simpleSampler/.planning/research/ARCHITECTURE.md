# DSP Architecture: O-simpleSampler

**CRITICAL CONTRACT:** This specification is immutable during Stages 1-4 implementation. Stage 1 cannot proceed without this file. Stage 2 (DSP) implements this exact architecture.

**Generated by:** Stage 0 Research & Planning
**Referenced by:** Stage 1 (Foundation), Stage 2 (DSP Implementation), Stage 3 (GUI)
**Purpose:** DSP specification for the sampler voice (source buffer → start/end region + loop/reverse → pitch engine Repitch|Stretch → Vintage → resonant LP filter → amp VCA), signal flow, JUCE 8 module usage, the resolved Repitch-vs-Stretch engine, sample-loading I/O, and the real-time-safe headline waveform visualization path.

**Plugin type:** Synth (Pedagogical Sampler Instrument), MIDI-in → audio-out, polyphonic (16 voices), WebView UI.

**Sibling lineage:** O-simpleSampler is the **fifth pedagogical "simple*" sibling** after the shipped O-simpleFM (v1.2.1), O-simpleAdditive (v1.0.2), O-simpleGrain (v1.0.1), and the Stage-0 O-simpleSubtractive. It is a **convergence of two existing siblings**: it reuses near-verbatim (a) from **O-simpleGrain** — the embedded-`.wav` built-in loading + decode/resample-off-thread + atomic source hot-swap, the `Source/dsp/LagrangeInterpolation.h` 4-point random-access fractional read, the O-GrainScatter `GrainScheduler` countdown + overlap-add grain pool (the **Stretch** engine), the rate-tracking one-pole anti-aliasing, the `webview-drop-streaming.js` macOS content-streaming drag-drop module + `juce::Base64::convertFromBase64`, the dual `juce_add_binary_data` NAMESPACE split (`UIBinaryData` + `BinaryData`), and the `VizRing`/FFT visualization tap; and (b) from **O-simpleSubtractive / O-Prism** — the resonant TPT low-pass (`juce::dsp::StateVariableTPTFilter` LP) + its closed-form magnitude curve, the per-voice `juce::ADSR` amp envelope, and the 16-voice `juce::Synthesiser` + custom `SynthesiserVoice` skeleton (O-simpleFM/O-Bassoon). The genuinely-new work is the **Repitch↔Stretch pitch engine** and its A/B framing, the **loop engine (equal-power crossfade + ping-pong + zero-cross snap)**, the **Vintage decimation+bit-crush macro**, and the **interactive waveform editor** (draggable region/loop handles + live playhead). All JUCE 8 classes below were verified against the local JUCE **8.0.9** source at `/Users/taylorbrook/JUCE/modules/` and against the shipped sibling sources in `plugins/`.

## Complexity & Research Summary

- **Complexity Tier:** **5–6** (MIDI synth with oscillators/voices escalated by file I/O + load-your-own sample streaming AND an interactive real-time waveform editor + filter-curve/scope visualizations). Net research depth: **DEEP**. Most of the *infrastructure* (voice model, amp ADSR, lock-free viz, WebView wiring, CMake synth + WebView2 flags, dual binary-data NAMESPACE) is inherited from O-simpleGrain/O-simpleSubtractive; most of the *sample-read + grain + loading* machinery is mined near-verbatim from O-simpleGrain/O-GrainScatter/O-MicrotonalSampler. The genuinely-new integration is the **two-mode pitch engine**, the **loop crossfade engine**, **Vintage**, and the **interactive waveform editor**.

- **HEADLINE DECISION — Stretch algorithm: synchronous-granular (SOLA-style) pitch shifter, NOT a phase vocoder.** The Pitch Mode toggle is the headline pitch/time-independence lesson (DSP-01). **Repitch** = a single continuous fractional-read **varispeed** (read increment = the key/tune ratio; pitch and duration coupled — the honest Mellotron/Fairlight behaviour). **Stretch** = a **synchronous-granular** engine reusing the O-simpleGrain/O-GrainScatter scheduler + overlap-add: the grain **source-position read head advances at 1× realtime** (the *time* axis, decoupled from pitch, so a held note keeps the region's natural duration) while **each grain is internally resampled by the key/tune ratio** (the *pitch* axis), overlap-added through a Hann window at ~2× overlap. **Phase vocoder is rejected for v1.0** — it adds STFT frame latency, "phasiness" artifacts, and has **no in-repo precedent**, whereas the granular engine is shipped, proven, lower-latency, has a designed offline-render-harness autocorrelation probe (per project memory: "granular pitch needs a single-grain autocorrelation probe"), and its mild "graininess" artifact profile is pedagogically honest. The internal grain parameters (size ~60 ms, Hann, 2× overlap, `MaxGrainsPerVoice=4`) are **fixed and hidden** behind the single toggle — the sampler hides the granular machinery (unlike O-simpleGrain, where it is the lesson). See **Architecture Decisions §1** and **Algorithm Details → Pitch Engine**.

- **Anti-aliasing decision (DSP-02 MUST — a teaching tool must not buzz on high keys):** **4-point Lagrange** fractional interpolation (`Source/dsp/LagrangeInterpolation.h`, random-access — required because both Repitch and grain reads hit arbitrary fractional positions) for the read, **plus a rate-tracking one-pole low-pass** applied when the playback `rate > 1` (`fc ≈ 0.5·fs/rate`) to band-limit before aliasing. This is O-simpleGrain decision #3 applied to both pitch modes. **Fallback:** interpolation-only (moderate transposition), then whole-engine 2× oversampling (last resort — adds CPU + latency). See **Architecture Decisions §2**.

- **Loop decision (DSP-03 MUST):** forward + ping-pong with an **equal-power (sin/cos) crossfade** across the seam (the tail approaching `loopEnd` crossfades with a head copy from `loopStart`), plus an **automatic zero-crossing snap** of the loop markers (cheap, computed off-thread when markers move) as a secondary click defense. `loopCrossfade` 0–500 ms (default 10 ms). See **Algorithm Details → Loop Engine**.

- **Vintage decision (DSP-04 MUST):** one macro = **sample-and-hold rate decimation** (`fsEff = lerp(fs, ~3 kHz, vintage)`) + **bit-depth quantization** (`bits = lerp(clean, ~8, vintage)`), SP-1200 lineage (26 kHz / 12-bit reference). **Fully bypassed at `vintage = 0`** (guarantees the "bit-for-bit clean" acceptance). Per-voice, placed **before the filter** (so the LP can tame decimation aliasing — the SP-1200-into-a-filter idiom). Mirrors the O-simpleAdditive bit-depth lesson. See **Algorithm Details → Vintage**.

- **Signal-path decision: the FULL chain is per-voice** (`source → region → pitch → Vintage → filter → VCA`) to match the brief's literal "single sampler voice" path and the UI's left-to-right teaching order exactly. The live filter-response curve (UI-03) is driven from the **lead voice** (most-recently-triggered) via `displayCutoffHz`/`displayK` atomics — one readable curve despite 16 voices (the O-simpleSubtractive lead-voice pattern). See **Architecture Decisions §5**.

- **Resonant filter decision:** a single per-voice resonant low-pass via **`juce::dsp::StateVariableTPTFilter<float>` in LP mode** (LINEAR — no self-oscillation requirement here, unlike O-simpleSubtractive). Being a TPT SVF, the same closed-form `Ω = tan(π·f/fs)/g` magnitude curve gives an **exact filter-response readout for free** (QUAL-02). See **Algorithm Details → Filter**.

- **Parameter resolution (open draft items):** **KEEP both `tune` (coarse st) and `fine` (cents)** — they teach distinct concepts (transpose vs detune) and are standard sampler controls (do NOT consolidate). **DEFER `velToFilter` to v1.1** (the per-voice filter makes it addable later without rearchitecting). **Key-tracking in Stretch** resolved: pitch tracks the key via grain resample while the time axis runs at 1× (held note keeps duration). **Polyphony = 16 voices** with default `juce::Synthesiser` stealing. **Source-length cap = 30 s** @ engine rate. Final v1.0 core = **21 parameters** (`Load…` is an action/native-function + custom state, NOT an APVTS param). See **Parameter Mapping**.

## Core Components

### Sampler Voice (SamplerVoice : juce::SynthesiserVoice)
- **JUCE Class:** Custom `SamplerVoice : juce::SynthesiserVoice` (owns the per-voice read state, a small preallocated `std::array<Grain, MaxGrainsPerVoice=4>` grain pool for Stretch, a per-grain/per-read `aaFilter` one-pole, a `juce::dsp::StateVariableTPTFilter<float>` LP, a per-voice S&H+quantize Vintage stage, and a `juce::ADSR` amp env).
- **Purpose:** Render one polyphonic sampler voice playing the shared source buffer tuned to its MIDI note: read the start/end region (with loop/reverse) at the pitch determined by `pitchMode`, apply Vintage → resonant LP → amp VCA. **NOT** `juce::SamplerVoice`/`SamplerSound` (those only do naive varispeed — no Stretch, no loop crossfade, no Vintage; rejected).
- **Parameters Affected:** all 21 params except `sourceSample` selection (which swaps the shared buffer).
- **Configuration:**
  - **Per-voice pitch ratio:** at note-on, `keyRatio = 2^((noteNumber − rootKey + tune + fine/100) / 12)` (root default C3/60). Pitch wheel ±2 st folds in. This ratio drives Repitch's read increment and Stretch's grain resample.
  - **Read state:** a `double readPos` (in source samples) within the `[startSamp, endSamp)` region; advances by the playback increment per sample (Repitch) or is the time-axis grain spawn position (Stretch). `reverse` negates the direction.
  - **Prepare:** custom `prepareToPlay(sampleRate, maxBlockSize)` sets `setCurrentPlaybackSampleRate` + `ampEnv.setSampleRate` + `lpFilter.prepare(spec)` **before** first `setParameters` (JUCE 8 `SynthesiserVoice` has NO virtual `prepareToPlay` — confirmed from O-simpleFM/O-simpleGrain voices).
  - **Note-on reset:** compute `keyRatio`, seat `readPos = startSamp` (or `endSamp` if reverse), clear grain pool + filter state, trigger amp ADSR with velocity.
  - **Voice lifetime:** active while `ampEnv.isActive()`; note-off → release tail → silence (a long loop sustains only while the note is held). Voice-stealing = `juce::Synthesiser` default (steal quietest/oldest).

### Source Buffer + Region/Loop/Reverse Read Engine
- **JUCE Class:** Custom (shared `juce::AudioBuffer<float>` source + per-voice region/loop read logic; equal-power crossfade like O-Freeze/O-GrainScatter loop handling).
- **Purpose:** Hold the (resampled-to-engine-rate) source recording and provide the region-limited, loopable, reversible read. THE raw material + the isolate/loop/reverse lessons (FUNC-04/05/06, DSP-03).
- **Parameters Affected:** `start`, `end`, `loopMode`, `loopStart`, `loopEnd`, `loopCrossfade`, `reverse`, `sourceSample`.
- **Configuration:**
  - **Region:** `startSamp = start% · sourceLen`, `endSamp = end% · sourceLen` (clamped `start < end`). Playback is confined to `[startSamp, endSamp)`.
  - **Loop:** `loopStart`/`loopEnd` are **% of the region** → absolute loop bounds `[loopAbsStart, loopAbsEnd)` inside `[startSamp, endSamp)`. `loopMode`:
    - **off:** play `start→end` once; voice fades on amp release.
    - **forward:** on reaching `loopAbsEnd`, wrap to `loopAbsStart` with an **equal-power crossfade** over `loopCrossfade` ms (see Algorithm Details).
    - **ping-pong:** reverse direction at each loop boundary, crossfading across the turnaround.
  - **Reverse:** negate the read increment over the whole region (independent of loop direction).
  - **Zero-crossing snap:** when `start/end/loopStart/loopEnd` change, snap the four markers to the nearest source zero-crossing (computed off the audio thread; published as int sample indices the voice reads) — secondary click defense.
  - **Hot-swap on `sourceSample`/load:** the source `AudioBuffer<float>` is swapped via an **atomic pointer swap** (double-buffer; new buffer built off-thread, atomically published; old reaped after a safe interval — O-MicrotonalSampler/O-simpleGrain pattern). The audio thread never touches a half-loaded buffer.

### Pitch Engine (Repitch varispeed | Stretch synchronous-granular) — THE headline component
- **JUCE Class:** Custom. **Repitch** = direct continuous fractional read (`LagrangeInterpolation.h` 4-pt). **Stretch** = O-GrainScatter `GrainScheduler` countdown + a preallocated `std::array<Grain,4>` overlap-add pool (mined from O-simpleGrain `GrainVoice.h`).
- **Purpose:** The defining sampler move — pitch/time independence as an audible + visible A/B toggle (DSP-01).
- **Parameters Affected:** `pitchMode`, `rootKey`, `tune`, `fine` (and MIDI note/velocity).
- **Configuration:**
  - **Repitch (default):** `readPos += keyRatio` per sample (within the region/loop). `keyRatio = 2` ⇒ one octave up reads twice as fast ⇒ half the duration (pitch+time coupled). One fractional read per sample (cheap). This is the workhorse path for the bulk of voices.
  - **Stretch:** the **time axis** (grain spawn source-position) advances at **1× realtime** through the region/loop (so duration is preserved regardless of key); the **pitch axis** is the grain's internal read increment `= keyRatio`. The scheduler fires a new grain every `grainPeriod = grainSize/2` (≈30 ms ⇒ 2× Hann overlap); each grain reads `grainSize` worth of source from the current time-axis position, resampled by `keyRatio`, windowed (Hann LUT), and overlap-added. Held note ⇒ same length, different pitch. Internal grain config is **fixed/hidden** (`grainSize≈60 ms`, Hann, 2× overlap, pool=4). See Architecture Decisions §1.
  - **Combination with loop/reverse:** the time-axis read head (Stretch) or the continuous read head (Repitch) is what loops/reverses; the pitch ratio rides on top of it.

### Anti-Aliasing Read (per read / per grain)
- **JUCE Class:** Custom (`lagrangeInterpolate` 4-pt random-access from `Source/dsp/LagrangeInterpolation.h` + a one-pole low-pass).
- **Purpose:** Clean fractional reads and **band-limited upward transposition so high keys do not alias/buzz** (DSP-02 — a hard teaching requirement).
- **Parameters Affected:** none directly (driven by the playback rate).
- **Configuration:** 4-pt Lagrange for the fractional source read (random access — correct for arbitrary positions; the stateful `juce::LagrangeInterpolator` streaming class is unsuitable). For `rate > 1` (transposed up), a one-pole low-pass `fc ≈ 0.5·fs/rate` band-limits before the read crosses Nyquist (`y += g·(x−y)`). At `rate ≤ 1` the filter is bypassed (no down-transposition aliasing). See Architecture Decisions §2.

### Vintage (sample-rate decimation + bit-depth reduction)
- **JUCE Class:** Custom per-voice (phase-accumulator S&H decimator + quantizer). Mirrors the O-simpleAdditive bit-depth lesson.
- **Purpose:** The SP-1200 lo-fi macro; clean at zero (DSP-04).
- **Parameters Affected:** `vintage`.
- **Configuration:**
  - **Decimation (S&H):** `fsEff = lerp(fs, FS_MIN≈3000 Hz, vintage)`; phase accumulator `phase += fsEff/fs; if (phase ≥ 1) { phase −= 1; held = x; } out = held;` (sample-and-hold ⇒ intentional aliasing grit).
  - **Bit-crush:** `bits = lerp(BITS_CLEAN, BITS_MIN≈8, vintage)`; `levels = 2^bits; out = round(out · levels/2) / (levels/2);`.
  - **Clean at zero:** when `vintage == 0`, **bypass entirely** (no S&H, no quantize ⇒ bit-for-bit clean — DSP-04 acceptance). SP-1200 reference ≈ 26.04 kHz / 12-bit; the macro sweeps from clean toward ~10-bit/~12 kHz musically and ~8-bit/~3–4 kHz at the extreme. No transcendental, no NaN risk (quantize + S&H are bounded); `isfinite` scrub downstream.

### Resonant Low-Pass Filter (per voice)
- **JUCE Class:** **`juce::dsp::StateVariableTPTFilter<float>`** (LP mode; verified `juce_dsp/processors/juce_StateVariableTPTFilter.h`). Linear TPT SVF — no self-oscillation needed.
- **Purpose:** Give the recording a tonal contour by removing highs (DSP-05).
- **Parameters Affected:** `filterCutoff`, `filterResonance`.
- **Configuration:** `setType(LowPass)`, `setCutoffFrequency(filterCutoff)`, `setResonance(0.5 … Qmax mapped from filterResonance%)`. Cutoff clamped below `0.45·fs`. Open by default (`filterCutoff` = 20 kHz). The **lead voice** publishes `displayCutoffHz`/`displayK` atomics so the message thread draws the exact closed-form magnitude curve (`Ω = tan(π·f/fs)/g`, LP `|H| = 1/|D|`) — same g/k math as audio ⇒ QUAL-02 by construction. (Per-voice `StateVariableTPTFilter` × 16 is acceptable; if profiling demands, drop to a 12-line custom TPT LP — the O-simpleSubtractive `SvfZDF` linear core.)

### Amplitude Envelope (Amp ADSR → VCA)
- **JUCE Class:** `juce::ADSR` + `juce::ADSR::Parameters` (verified `juce_audio_basics/utilities/juce_ADSR.h`).
- **Purpose:** Shape per-voice loudness (playable contour) and govern voice lifetime (DSP-06).
- **Parameters Affected:** `ampAttack`, `ampDecay`, `ampSustain`, `ampRelease`, `velToAmp`.
- **Configuration:** times 0–5 s (skew ~0.35), sustain 0–1. `velToAmp` (0–100%, default 50%) scales the note-on level by velocity: `level = (1 − velToAmp) + velToAmp · velocity`. Voice active while `ampEnv.isActive()`. `setSampleRate` before first `setParameters`. Mirrors O-simpleGrain/O-simpleSubtractive exactly.

### Output Stage
- **JUCE Class:** `juce::SmoothedValue<float>` gain + final block `std::isfinite` scrub.
- **Purpose:** Master trim (`outputLevel`) + suite-wide NaN insurance + summed-voice headroom.
- **Configuration:** dB→linear, 20 ms smoothing; soft-safety + `isfinite` scrub after summing voices.

### Visualization Tap (Waveform editor + Playhead + Filter curve + Scope)
- **JUCE Class:** A `std::atomic<double> displayPlayhead` (lead-voice read position) + `displayCutoffHz`/`displayK`/`displayLoop*` atomics for the live overlays; the **static waveform** (min/max peak bins) is computed off-thread on load and pushed once; an optional `VizRing` + message-thread `juce::dsp::FFT` output scope/spectrum (reused from O-simpleGrain `VizAnalyzer.h`). FFT runs on the **message thread** (editor Timer 30 Hz), never the audio thread.
- **Purpose:** Feed the headline interactive waveform editor (UI-01), the Repitch-vs-Stretch playhead behaviour (UI-02), the filter-response curve + amp-ADSR (UI-03), and the optional output scope.
- **Configuration:** see **Visualization Data Path** below.

## Processing Chain

```
MIDI in
  ↓
[processor, per block] read APVTS atomics → push to each voice via setParams(...)
  (region/loop bounds in samples + zero-cross-snapped markers; pitchMode; vintage; filter; amp; output)
  ↓
juce::Synthesiser (16 × SamplerVoice)
  │   per voice:
  │     keyRatio = 2^((note − rootKey + tune + fine/100)/12)   ← rootKey, tune, fine, MIDI note
  │     ── REGION/LOOP/REVERSE read head (within [startSamp,endSamp)) ──   ← start, end, loopMode, loopStart, loopEnd, reverse
  │        loop seam → equal-power crossfade over loopCrossfade ms          ← loopCrossfade (DSP-03)
  │     ── PITCH ENGINE ──
  │        Repitch: readPos += keyRatio ; src = lagrange(readPos)            (pitch+time coupled)
  │        Stretch: time-axis advances 1× ; grains resampled by keyRatio ;   (pitch/time independent, DSP-01)
  │                 Hann overlap-add (pool=4, ~2× overlap)
  │        src = aaOnePole(src, rate)   (band-limit if rate>1)               ← DSP-02 anti-alias
  │     ── VINTAGE ──  src = bitcrush(decimateSH(src, fsEff), bits)  (bypass at 0) ← vintage (DSP-04)
  │     ── FILTER ──   src = svfLP(src, cutoff, resonance)                   ← filterCutoff, filterResonance (DSP-05)
  │     ── VCA ──      voiceOut = src · ampEnv.getNextSample() · velLevel    ← amp ADSR + velToAmp (DSP-06)
  ↓ (sum of active voices)
Output gain (SmoothedValue, dB→lin)  ← outputLevel
  ↓
NaN scrub (std::isfinite insurance)
  ↓
[TAPS — audio thread, lock-free, no alloc, no FFT]
   lead-voice readPos      → std::atomic<double> displayPlayhead          (waveform playhead UI-01/02)
   lead-voice cutoff/Q     → displayCutoffHz / displayK atomics            (filter curve UI-03)
   mono-sum (optional)     → VizRing                                       (output scope/spectrum)
  ↓
Audio out (stereo, mono source duplicated)

   ── message thread (Editor Timer 30 Hz) ──
   displayPlayhead + loop/region params  → "playheadUpdate" (live read position; Repitch vs Stretch reads differently)
   closed-form filter magnitude (Ω=tan(πf/fs)/g, same g/k as audio) → "filterCurveUpdate"  (UI-03)
   amp-env value (atomic)                → "envUpdate"  (amp-ADSR display, UI-03)
   VizRing → copy scope window (BEFORE FFT) → scope + optional FFT(4096) → "scopeUpdate"/"spectrumUpdate"
        → emitEventIfBrowserIsVisible(...) → WebView JS draws over the static waveform
   (static waveform peaks pushed ONCE on load → "waveformUpdate")
```

**Routing notes:**
- **The full chain is per-voice** (`source → region → pitch → Vintage → filter → VCA`) — matches the brief's literal signal path and the UI's left-to-right teaching order. No global insert except the master output gain.
- **No feedback loop.** A sampler is feed-forward (loop playback is buffer re-reading, NOT a DSP feedback path) — removes the single hardest stability class (vs O-simpleFM's DX7 feedback). Lower core-DSP risk than the FM/subtractive siblings.
- **The live filter curve is driven from the LEAD voice** (most-recently-triggered active voice) via `displayCutoffHz`/`displayK` atomics, so one readable curve represents the dominant heard timbre despite 16 voices (O-simpleSubtractive pattern).
- **The waveform playhead is the headline visual:** the lead voice's read position is published every block. **In Repitch it moves at the pitch-coupled rate** (fast on high notes), **in Stretch at 1×** (constant) — so the playhead *itself* makes the pitch/time-independence lesson visible (UI-02) for free.
- **Visualization is read-only taps**, never in the audio return path.

## System Architecture

### File I/O System (built-in embedding + load-your-own)

**File types supported:** `.wav`, `.aiff`, `.flac` (via `AudioFormatManager::registerBasicFormats()`); built-ins ship as embedded `.wav`.

**Loading strategy:** Off-audio-thread decode + resample into an `AudioBuffer<float>`, then **atomic pointer-swap** to publish to the engine. The audio thread only ever reads a fully-built, published source buffer.

**Built-in embedding (COMPAT-02 — dual binary-data targets):**
- Curated built-ins embedded via a SECOND `juce_add_binary_data` target. **CRITICAL:** the embedded-samples target and the WebView-UI-resources target collide on the default `BinaryData` namespace unless given distinct `NAMESPACE` *and* `HEADER_NAME` (the O-simpleGrain Stage-3.1 lesson — a distinct `HEADER_NAME` alone is NOT enough). Mirror O-simpleGrain exactly:
  ```cmake
  juce_add_binary_data(O-simpleSampler_UIResources  NAMESPACE UIBinaryData HEADER_NAME UIBinaryData.h SOURCES <ui files>)
  juce_add_binary_data(O-simpleSampler_Samples      NAMESPACE BinaryData   HEADER_NAME BinaryData.h   SOURCES <.wav files>)
  ```
- Decode: `auto reader = formatManager.createReaderFor(std::make_unique<juce::MemoryInputStream>(BinaryData::xxx_wav, (size_t)BinaryData::xxx_wavSize, false));` → `reader->read(&buffer, ...)` → resample to engine rate (`juce::LagrangeInterpolator` offline ratio resample). Both `createReaderFor` overloads verified locally and in-use in O-simpleGrain.
- Done at construction / on `sourceSample` change, off the audio thread.
- **Per-sample default root key:** each built-in carries a default root note (metadata table, e.g. piano→C3, vocal→A3); selecting a built-in seeds `rootKey` to its default (overridable). Built-in set ≈ 4–6 curated found-sounds (e.g. piano hit, vocal "ah", Mellotron-ish flute, vinyl/found texture).

**Load-your-own (FUNC-03) — macOS WebView content-streaming drag-drop + file-picker fallback:**
- **macOS drag-drop:** WKWebView sandboxes file paths, so **reuse the shipped O-simpleGrain `webview-drop-streaming.js` module** (itself the O-MicrotonalSampler v1.0.4 pattern): JS `dataTransfer.items[0].webkitGetAsEntry()` → `FileReader` → ArrayBuffer → `btoa()` base64 → stream through JUCE `NativeFunction`s (`dropSampleStart`/`dropSampleChunk`/`dropSampleCommit`) into a session temp dir, then decode+resample as above.
- **CRITICAL gotcha (project memory):** decode the streamed base64 with **`juce::Base64::convertFromBase64(OutputStream&, StringRef)`**, NOT `juce::MemoryBlock::fromBase64Encoding` (JUCE's own non-standard `<size>.<alt>` format — silently rejects standard `btoa()` output). In-use at O-MicrotonalSampler `PluginProcessor.cpp` and O-simpleGrain.
- **File-picker fallback:** `juce::FileChooser` (async, message thread) for users/platforms without working drag-drop.
- **Source-length cap (30 s @ engine rate):** truncate longer loads (UI notice) to keep the waveform display + read math + memory bounded.

**JUCE classes:** `juce::AudioFormatManager`, `juce::AudioFormatReader`, `juce::WavAudioFormat`/`juce::AiffAudioFormat`/`juce::FlacAudioFormat`, `juce::MemoryInputStream`, `juce::LagrangeInterpolator` (resample), `juce::Base64::convertFromBase64`, `juce::FileChooser`, `juce::WebBrowserComponent::NativeFunction`.

**Thread safety:** all decode/resample on the message/background thread; built source `AudioBuffer<float>` published via atomic pointer swap (double-buffer; old reaped after a safe interval). Audio thread reads only the published buffer (no locks, no I/O).

**Error handling:** missing/invalid file → keep previous source + UI notice; unsupported format → skip + notice; oversized → truncate to 30 s + notice.

### MIDI Routing
- **Bus:** MIDI-in → stereo audio-out (no audio input). **`IS_SYNTH TRUE` + `NEEDS_MIDI_INPUT TRUE`** in CMake (critical-pattern #22 — omitting yields a silent plugin). `BusesProperties` output-only.

- **Pitch wheel:** ±2 semitone bend (sibling pattern).

## Algorithm Details

### Pitch Engine (the resolved Repitch↔Stretch decision)

`keyRatio = 2^((noteNumber − rootKey + tune + fine/100) / 12)` (root C3 ⇒ sample plays at recorded pitch on C3).

**Repitch (varispeed):** a single continuous fractional read. `readPos += keyRatio` per sample within the region/loop; `src = lagrangeInterpolate(buf, readPos)`. Octave up ⇒ `keyRatio=2` ⇒ reads twice as fast ⇒ pitch up AND duration halved (coupled). This is the honest Mellotron/Fairlight behaviour and the cheap default path.

**Stretch (synchronous-granular / SOLA pitch shift):** decouple time from pitch.
- **Time axis (duration):** a `double timePos` advances through the region/loop at **1× realtime** (`timePos += 1.0` per sample, independent of `keyRatio`). This is what loops/reverses, and what the waveform playhead shows — so duration is preserved regardless of key.
- **Pitch axis:** the scheduler fires a grain every `grainHop = grainSize·(1−overlap)` (≈30 ms for 2× Hann overlap). Each grain captures its **start position = current `timePos`** and reads `grainSize` worth of source **resampled by `keyRatio`** (`grain.readPos += keyRatio`), windowed by the Hann LUT, overlap-added:
  ```
  on hop:  spawn grain { srcStart = timePos; readPos = timePos; phase = 0; }
  per active grain/sample:
     env = hannLUT[phase];  s = lagrange(buf, grain.readPos) · env;  out += s;
     grain.readPos += keyRatio;  grain.phase += 1/grainSamples;
     if (phase ≥ 1) grain.active = false;
  ```
- **Result:** a held note keeps the region's natural duration (time axis at 1×) while pitch tracks the key (grain resample). **Repitch↔Stretch toggling on a sustained sample is audibly + visibly obvious** (DSP-01 acceptance) — the playhead speed and the perceived duration change while pitch stays keyed.
- Internal grain config is **fixed/hidden** (`grainSize≈60 ms`, Hann, 2× overlap, pool=4). Artifact profile: mild "graininess"/transient smearing at extreme stretch (pedagogically honest; phase-vocoder "phasiness" avoided).

### Anti-aliasing (DSP-02)
Fractional read via 4-pt **Lagrange** (`lagrangeInterpolate(ym1,y0,y1,y2,frac)` — random-access, stateless, correct for arbitrary positions; the stateful `juce::LagrangeInterpolator` streaming class is unsuitable for arbitrary grain reads). For up-transposed reads (`rate = keyRatio > 1`) a **one-pole low-pass** `fc ≈ 0.5·fs/rate` band-limits before Nyquist (`g` from `fc`; `y += g·(x−y)`). At `rate ≤ 1` bypass. Keeps high keys clean without global oversampling (zero added latency). Fallback: interpolation-only, then whole-engine 2× OS only if measured aliasing exceeds the harness budget.

### Loop Engine (DSP-03)
- **Forward:** when `readPos` enters the last `xfadeSamp = loopCrossfade·fs/1000` samples before `loopAbsEnd`, mix a **second read head** starting from `loopAbsStart` with an **equal-power** crossfade: `out = a·cos(θ) + b·sin(θ)`, `θ = (0→π/2)` across the fade; at the seam the primary head jumps to `loopAbsStart + xfadeSamp` and the cycle repeats. Equal-power keeps perceived loudness constant across the seam.
- **Ping-pong:** at each loop boundary, negate the read direction; crossfade across the turnaround the same way.
- **Zero-crossing snap:** when any of `start/end/loopStart/loopEnd` change, snap to the nearest source zero-crossing (sign change between adjacent samples), computed off the audio thread and published as int sample indices — a cheap secondary defense so even `loopCrossfade=0` is reasonably clean. The crossfade is the primary defense.
- **Reverse interaction:** `reverse` negates the base read increment independently; loop logic operates on the (possibly reversed) direction.

### Vintage (DSP-04 — SP-1200 macro)
S&H decimation (`fsEff = lerp(fs, 3000, vintage)`, phase-accumulator hold) + bit-crush (`bits = lerp(clean, 8, vintage)`, `out = round(x·L/2)/(L/2)`, `L=2^bits`). **`vintage==0` ⇒ full bypass** (bit-for-bit clean — DSP-04 acceptance). Placed per-voice **before the filter** (LP tames decimation aliasing — the SP-1200-into-filter idiom). No NaN/denormal risk (bounded ops); covered by the block `isfinite` scrub.

### Filter (DSP-05) + closed-form curve (QUAL-02)
`juce::dsp::StateVariableTPTFilter<float>` LP per voice: `g = tan(π·fc/fs)`, `k = 1/Q(resonance)`. The lead voice publishes `displayCutoffHz`/`displayK`; the message thread draws the **exact** magnitude from the same g/k: `Ω = tan(π·f/fs)/g; |H_LP| = 1/sqrt((1−Ω²)² + (kΩ)²); dB = 20·log10|H|`. Same math as audio ⇒ the curve matches what's heard by construction.

### Amp ADSR + velocity (DSP-06)
Per-voice `juce::ADSR`; `velLevel = (1−velToAmp) + velToAmp·velocity`; voice lifetime gated on `ampEnv.isActive()`. Velocity→amp is the responsiveness the class describes.

### Headroom / clipping
16 voices sum; trim with `outputLevel`; soft-safety + block `std::isfinite` scrub. (No overlap explosion — Stretch's 2× Hann overlap is gain-normalized by the window's constant-overlap-add property.)

### Preset Recipes (FUNC-07 — concept-isolating tour)
**Star** = the one move each isolates.
| Preset | Settings | ★ Star concept |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| Raw One-Shot | loop off, full region, Repitch, clean | a recording made playable from a key |
| Tuned Across the Keyboard | Repitch, root set, play a melody | one sound → tunable instrument (FUNC-01) |
| Looped Pad | loop forward, short region, xfade 30 ms, long release | a short sound sustained as a held pad (FUNC-05) |

### Processing Order (load-bearing)
1. **Processor, once per block:** read APVTS atomics; convert region/loop %→samples + apply zero-cross-snapped marker indices; push to each voice via `setParams(...)` (O-simpleFM block-push). Voices never read APVTS directly.
2. **Per note-on:** compute `keyRatio`; seat the read head at `startSamp` (or `endSamp` if reverse); clear grain pool + filter state; trigger amp ADSR with velocity.
3. **Per voice, per sample:** region/loop read head → pitch engine (Repitch continuous read OR Stretch grain overlap-add) → AA → Vintage (bypass at 0) → LP filter → VCA.
4. **Sum voices → output gain.**
5. **Taps last** (post-gain, copy-only): `displayPlayhead` (lead voice readPos), `displayCutoffHz`/`displayK` (lead voice), `VizRing` (samples). Static waveform peaks pushed once on load.

### Parameter Interactions
- **`pitchMode` × everything pitch.** Repitch couples pitch+duration; Stretch decouples them. The single most important interaction — the headline lesson (DSP-01).
- **`start`/`end` × `loopStart`/`loopEnd`.** Loop bounds are % of the region, so moving Start/End rescales the loop; the waveform editor must show loop handles *inside* the region shade.
- **`loopMode` × `loopCrossfade` × `reverse`.** Crossfade smooths the seam; ping-pong + reverse compose (reverse flips the base direction, ping-pong flips at boundaries).
- **`vintage` × `filterCutoff`.** Vintage precedes the filter, so the LP can tame decimation aliasing (SP-1200-into-filter).
- **`rootKey`/`tune`/`fine` × MIDI note.** All fold into one `keyRatio`; `rootKey` is seeded per built-in but user-overridable.
- **velocity × amp (`velToAmp`).** Harder = louder by the routing amount.

## Architecture Decisions

### 1. Stretch = synchronous-granular (SOLA) pitch shifter, NOT a phase vocoder — THE headline decision
**Decision:** Stretch decouples time from pitch by reusing the O-simpleGrain/O-GrainScatter grain scheduler + overlap-add: the time-axis read head advances at 1× realtime (duration preserved) while each grain is resampled by `keyRatio` (pitch tracks the key), overlap-added through a fixed Hann window at ~2× overlap (grain ~60 ms, pool 4, hidden internals). Repitch is a plain continuous fractional-read varispeed.
**Rationale:**
- **In-repo, shipped, proven:** the grain engine ships in O-simpleGrain v1.0.1 / O-GrainScatter v2.4.0; reuse near-verbatim.
- **Lower latency + simpler than a phase vocoder:** no STFT frame latency, no FFT bin management, no phase-unwrapping.
- **Honest, teachable artifact profile:** mild graininess at extreme stretch is pedagogically acceptable; phase-vocoder "phasiness" is harder to explain and worse for transient material.
- **Validation already designed:** the offline render-harness single-grain **autocorrelation** probe (project memory: "granular pitch needs a single-grain autocorrelation probe; the grain-rate comb confounds spectral probes") directly validates Stretch pitch + duration independence.
- **The playhead visual is free:** time axis at 1× vs Repitch's pitch-coupled rate makes the lesson *visible* (UI-02) without extra UI work.
**Alternatives considered:** (a) **Phase vocoder** — best fidelity for large stretch, BUT adds latency + phasiness + has no in-repo precedent → **rejected for v1.0, deferred to v1.1 as an optional "HQ Stretch" mode**. (b) **WSOLA/PSOLA** — pitch-synchronous overlap-add tracks pitch marks for cleaner monophonic stretch, BUT requires pitch detection and is monophonic-leaning → unnecessary complexity for a teaching tool; the synchronous-granular approach is the pragmatic middle.
**Tradeoffs accepted:** mild grain artifacts at extreme settings; hidden internal grain params (intentional — the sampler hides the machinery the granular sibling exposes).

### 2. Anti-aliasing: 4-pt Lagrange + rate-tracking one-pole (NOT global oversampling)
**Decision:** 4-pt Lagrange fractional read + per-read one-pole low-pass (`fc=0.5fs/rate`) when `rate>1`. **Rationale:** reads hit arbitrary fractional positions (continuous Repitch read + grain reads), so a random-access interpolator is required; the rate-tracking one-pole band-limits up-transposition cheaply (the Clouds/Beads approach), zero added latency. A teaching tool must not buzz on high keys (DSP-02). **Alternatives:** whole-engine 2× oversampling (CPU + latency — fallback only); stateful `juce::LagrangeInterpolator` streaming class (unsuitable for arbitrary grain positions). **Tradeoff:** not brickwall AA — inaudible for a teaching instrument, far cheaper.

### 3. Custom `SamplerVoice`, NOT `juce::SamplerSound`/`SamplerVoice`
**Decision:** a custom `SynthesiserVoice`. **Rationale:** JUCE's built-in `Sampler` does only naive varispeed playback — no Stretch, no loop crossfade, no ping-pong, no Vintage, no per-voice filter. **Alternative rejected:** built-in Sampler (cannot meet DSP-01/03/04/05). **Tradeoff:** more code, but it's a thin reuse of the O-simpleFM/O-simpleGrain voice skeleton.

### 4. Embed built-ins as `.wav` via a SECOND `juce_add_binary_data` target with a distinct NAMESPACE
**Decision:** samples target `NAMESPACE BinaryData HEADER_NAME BinaryData.h`; UI-resources target `NAMESPACE UIBinaryData HEADER_NAME UIBinaryData.h` (copy O-simpleGrain). **Rationale:** frictionless projector demo + avoids the duplicate-`BinaryData`-symbol link failure (O-simpleGrain Stage-3.1). **Alternative rejected:** loose files on disk (paths break on install); single binary-data target (can't hold both without the namespace split). **Tradeoff:** two targets — trivial.

### 5. Full per-voice chain (source→region→pitch→Vintage→filter→VCA); lead-voice filter curve
**Decision:** the entire chain is per-voice; the live filter curve is driven from the lead (most-recently-triggered) voice. **Rationale:** matches the brief's literal "single sampler voice" signal path and the UI's left-to-right teaching order exactly (no pedagogically-dishonest global inserts mid-path); the lead-voice curve gives one readable curve despite 16 voices (O-simpleSubtractive pattern). **Alternative considered:** global filter/Vintage after the voice sum — simpler curve but breaks the per-note signal-path story and complicates the per-voice playback contour. **Tradeoff:** 16× filter/Vintage instances — CPU-light; accepted for pedagogical honesty.

### 6. Reuse the shipped `webview-drop-streaming.js` content-streaming module for load-your-own
**Decision:** reuse O-simpleGrain's drag-drop module + `Base64::convertFromBase64`; `FileChooser` fallback. **Rationale:** WKWebView sandboxes file paths; the content-streaming pattern is the only thing that works on macOS WebView and it's already shipped. **Alternative rejected:** native file-path drag-drop (silently yields nothing on macOS WKWebView — documented). **Tradeoff:** ~33% base64 streaming overhead — acceptable for short found-sounds.

## Special Considerations

- **Thread safety:** APVTS atomics for params; `displayPlayhead`/`displayCutoffHz`/`displayK`/env atomics + `VizRing` for audio→UI; **atomic pointer swap** for source-buffer hot-swap; no locks on the audio thread; UI never calls processing code (critical-pattern #5). Per-voice `juce::Random` not needed (Stretch grains are deterministic — no spray).
- **Performance:** 16 voices × (1 Lagrange read [Repitch] or ≤4 grain reads [Stretch] + optional one-pole + S&H/quantize + 1 SVF LP + 1 ADSR) per sample — light, comparable to O-simpleSubtractive (16 × osc+SVF+2 ADSR). Most voices run the cheap Repitch path. Optional 4096 FFT @ 30 Hz on the message thread is cheap. Well under one core at 48 kHz; no oversampling in v1.0.
- **Denormals:** `juce::ScopedNoDenormals` at the top of `processBlock`; per-voice + block `std::isfinite` scrub. Filter + ADSR tails warrant the guard; no feedback loop so denormal exposure is otherwise low.
- **Sample rate:** the source buffer is **resampled to the engine rate at load** (so read math is sample-rate-clean); region/loop sample bounds, `loopCrossfade·fs/1000`, Vintage `fsEff`, and AA `0.5fs/rate` use current `fs`; recompute on `prepareToPlay`. Window LUT is sample-rate-independent (indexed by grain phase). ADSR/filter `setSampleRate`/`prepare` before first `setParameters`.
- **Latency:** **Zero** added latency in v1.0 (no oversampling, no lookahead) — call `setLatencySamples(0)` (NB: `getLatencySamples()` is **non-virtual** in JUCE 8 — do NOT override). If the 2× OS AA fallback is ever needed, report its latency then.
- **Zipper noise (QUAL-01):** `SmoothedValue` on `filterCutoff`/`filterResonance`/`outputLevel` and on `start`/`end`/`loop*` marker moves (so dragging a handle doesn't click); equal-power loop crossfade + zero-cross snap; never hard-jump the read head or reset grain phase mid-grain. The ONLY intentional grit is **Vintage** (a feature, not a bug — DSP-04).
