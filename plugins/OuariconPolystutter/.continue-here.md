# OuariconPolystutter - Continue Here

## Current State
- **Version:** 1.1.18
- **Status:** Audio working, but clicks at repeat boundaries need fixing
- **Date:** 2026-01-17

## Issue Summary
Audio stuttering is functional (fixed in v1.1.18), but there are still audible clicks at repeat boundaries. This is the original issue that v1.1.17 attempted to fix, but that fix caused a worse regression (no audio at all).

## Problem History

### v1.1.17 Attempt (REVERTED)
Tried to fix boundary clicks by applying different gains to old/new audio during crossfade:
- `oldGain = currentGain * volumeLevel`
- `newGain = currentGain * decayAmount * volumeLevel`

This was supposed to smooth the gain transition during crossfade so that when `startNewRepeat()` applied decay, the audio level would already match. **But this caused complete audio loss** - only clicks were heard with no actual stutter audio.

### v1.1.18 Fix (CURRENT)
Reverted to unconditional gain application after crossfade (like v1.1.13). Audio works again, but clicks are back.

## Root Cause of Clicks
The clicks occur because:
1. During crossfade: audio is blended at `currentGain` level
2. After crossfade ends, `startNewRepeat()` is called
3. `startNewRepeat()` applies decay: `currentGain *= decayAmount`
4. First sample after crossfade is at the new (lower) gain
5. This causes a sudden ~10% volume drop = audible click

## Potential Fix Approaches

### Approach 1: Apply decay BEFORE crossfade ends
Move the decay application from `startNewRepeat()` to happen during the crossfade, so by the time crossfade completes, `currentGain` already reflects the decayed value.

### Approach 2: Smooth gain transition in crossfade
Apply gain smoothly during crossfade:
- At crossfade start: use `currentGain`
- At crossfade end: use `currentGain * decayAmount`
- Interpolate between them based on crossfade progress

**Key insight:** The v1.1.17 approach was correct in concept but had an implementation bug that caused the if/else to not cover all code paths. Need to ensure gain is ALWAYS applied while still handling the smooth transition.

### Approach 3: Post-crossfade gain smoothing
After crossfade completes, add a short gain ramp from old gain to new gain over a few samples.
