## Summary (what the plan must absorb)

1. **Topology.** Stream s reads and writes slot s only, so two streams never share a tape. Feedback per stream = split the shared control ramps (`fbRamp`, `diffRamp`, `satDRamp`, `fbLowLogRamp` / `fbHighLogRamp`, `chainLowHz` / `chainHighHz`, mod substitutes) into four sets.
2. **Reverb Post** = one tank per stream ("tank A") on that stream's audible term, before the stream sum. The wet knee stays once, after the sum, before `output_gain`.
3. **Reverb Pre needs a second tank per stream.** The tap (coherent grain law, mean over voices, level cap, no pan) and the audible term are different signals, so one mono-input tank cannot serve both. Recommended: tank A stays on the audible term in both modes; Pre enables a loop-side "tank B" on the tap, before the chain (`Source/dsp/Engine.cpp:3745→3765`). Pre ↔ Post = tank B's `enabled` atomic; the module's 50 ms fade is the crossfade. `reverb-unit` 1.2.0 needs no change (no mutable statics, about 393 KB per tank).
4. **Boundedness in Pre holds by the writer**, not the AGC: the writer clamp plus the convex envelope (`Source/dsp/GiRecordHead.h:181-198`) give |w| ≤ max(|wc|, 0.7) for any finite return. The AGC alone does not (1 ms attack; probe wet +4 to +6 dB RMS on noise). A finite guard before tank B is mandatory: a NaN in a tank is permanent.
   - Behaviour to check by ear: in Pre the loop can sit at the ceiling instead of decaying, and the head keeps writing for the tail's length after note-off (up to about 20 s at Size 100).
5. **Feedback toggle Off** = fold into the head's run target (`Engine.cpp:3859`): a per-stream Freeze with a 20 ms ramp, no writes, and a routed Amount cannot lift it. Amount 0 stays a bitwise no-op through the parked-head skip (`:3873`, `:3895`).

| Difference | Tap | Audible term | Size |
|---|---|---|---|

#### Options evaluated

- **(a) One tank on the stream's audible term; Pre tap = dry tap blended with the tank's wet, renormalised.** Needs the wet-only signal (the module mixes dry/wet in place: engine-side mixing with the mix atomic pinned to 1, or a module 1.3.0 API). The renormaliser must undo four conventions at the tank **output**: `aud·Σe·g_fb / (vgain·max(1,level)·max(1,Σe)·Σe·g_out)`. It is undefined after release (Σe → 0, tail still ringing) and pumps: a 16-voice chord's tail rises up to 24 dB in the tap as the envelopes release. FL2 (runaway guard) holds only while notes are held. Every Post render with reverb > 0 changes arithmetic (engine-side mix). **Rejected.**
- **(b) Second tank per stream, fed by the tap itself, inside the loop; the audible tank is untouched.** Exact tap convention at the tank input, so the D7 mean argument is intact up to the tank's own gain. Both tanks use the module natively, in place. Cost: one extra tank per stream that is in Pre. **Recommended.**
- **(c) One tank moved between two insertion points.** In the Pre position the tank is fed the tap, so the audible wet would have to be derived from a mean-normalised, pan-less, `g_fb` signal: chords' reverb would stop growing with polyphony and the stream pan would be lost. A Pre ↔ Post switch would also need a tank flush or a dual-position crossfade. **Rejected.**

#### Recommended definition

Two `ReverbUnit`s per stream, same Mix / Size / Damp atomics:

- **Tank A (audible)**: on stream s's term, before the stream sum. Always the "Post position". Identical in both modes, so the forward sound does not change when the switch is flipped.
- **Tank B (loop)**: on `tapBuf` in `runChains`, before `chain[s].process`. Runs only in Pre. The module's own dry/wet is the tap's dry/wet: Mix 100 % writes tails only.

POST (default; today's behaviour per stream)

PRE (sN_reverb_pre On)

### A · Q2 — Boundedness in Pre mode

#### The bound

- The writer clamps the return to ±`kAgcCeil` (0.7) and then clamps the written value into `[min(wc,rw), max(wc,rw)]` (`GiRecordHead.h:181-198`). So `|w| ≤ max(|wc|, 0.7)` for **any** finite return. A non-finite `w` is replaced by `wc`. **V**
- That bound does not depend on anything upstream of the writer. With tank B before the chain, the tape stays ≤ max(pristine peak, 0.7) at Mix 100 %, Size 100 %. **V** (by code)

#### Freeze and Reset today

#### Amount 0 is a bitwise no-op — where it is guaranteed

#### Feedback toggle On ↔ Off

Fold `sN_fb_on` into the head's run target: `target = gp.s[s].fbOn && ! gp.freeze && …` (`:3859`).

- Off is then a per-stream Freeze: 20 ms `run` ramp, park, no writes. Amount is not touched.
- It cannot be lifted by modulation of `sN_feedback` (a scale on the base value could be, which is why this is not done on `fbRamp`).
- No new ramp, no change to `RecordHead::writeCell`.
- Off from load: `run.reset(0)`, parked, zero writes ever.

**A** (design; the mechanism is V)

#### Pre ↔ Post

The switch only changes `gateB_s`. Tank A and the audible path do not move.

- Post → Pre: tank B cold-starts (`clearTank`, snap, 50 ms active-gain fade-in, `ReverbUnit.h:186-190, 205-215`). The tap goes from dry to `dry·(1−w) + wet·w` over 50 ms.
- Pre → Post: 50 ms fade-out, then the unit idles and stops touching the buffer.
- Tank state: nothing is kept across a switch (the module never resumes a stale tail). No crossfade code in the engine, no flush call.
- The output cannot click at the switch: the only path to the output is tape → later grain reads, through the chain's filters, the convex write and the 20 ms λ / run ramps.

Under Freeze: tank B keeps running (like the chains), its output is discarded by the parked head; a switch under Freeze changes nothing audible or written.

At Reset All: tank B holds the old tape's tail and, with the `returnPk` run rule, would write it into the fresh pristine copy even with no key held. Recommended: a per-stream `preHold_s` countdown of `rampCellsFor(fs, 60 ms)` cells, set at a Reset take of slot s (`:1227`), forcing `gateB_s` off so the module fades and idles; tank B then restarts cold. Tank B is not audible directly, so this has no audible side effect. Tank A's audible tail is untouched. **A**

### A · Q5 — Reverb vs mute / solo / enable ramps; the wet knee

#### Today

- `aud` is inside the stream term, before the stream sum and before the reverb (`Engine.cpp:4055, 4061`). A stream whose `aud` is settled at 0 is skipped (`:3949-3959, 4050-4051`). **V**
- So a muted stream stops feeding the output tank in 10 ms and its tail **rings out** in the shared tank. **V**
- The tap also carries `aud` (`:3734-3735`) and the run rule's voice term needs `audibleTarget` (`:3858`): a muted stream stops writing once its chain return has decayed. **V**
- `wetKnee` sits after the reverb on the summed bus, then `output_gain` (`:4129-4149`). It is identity ≤ −6 dBFS and bounded by `kKneeA` for any finite input (`forked/OutputLaw.h:182-187`). **V**

#### Options for a muted / un-soloed / disabled stream's tail

| | Behaviour | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| A. Ring out | Tank A keeps running on zero input until the tail dies. Matches today. | A muted or disabled stream burns a tank; solo leaves up to 12 s of the other streams' tails; in Pre the tail keeps writing unless gate B is separately tied to `aud`. |
| **B. Fade and idle (recommended)** | `gateA_s = audibleTarget`. Dry cuts in 10 ms (today's ramp); the module fades that stream's wet over its 50 ms and idles; un-mute restarts a cold tank with a 50 ms fade-in. | The stream must stay in the sum while the fade runs: a `revTail_s` countdown of `rampCellsFor(fs, 50 ms) + 2` cells, re-armed at every tick where gate A is on with mix > 0.001, keeps `audible[s]` true. |
| C. Keep running, multiply the wet by `aud` | Instant restore on un-mute. | Needs the wet-only signal (engine-side mixing) and still burns the tank. |

B is the one consistent with the tap: a muted stream is silent, stops feeding its loop tank and stops writing (gate B carries the same `audibleTarget` term). It is also what keeps the typical CPU budget: D64 copies each preset's reverb to all four streams, including disabled ones, and only B idles those tanks. **A**

This changes one behaviour users can hear: today a muted stream's tail rings out; with B it fades in 50 ms. Needs the user's sign-off.

Rule that follows from B: `reverbA[s].process` and `reverbB[s].process` are called for **every** stream on **every** piece (zeros for a skipped stream). An idle unit returns immediately; a unit that is never called cannot finish its fade, stays `running`, and resumes a stale tail on un-mute (`ReverbUnit.h:186-197`). **V** (module logic)

#### The wet safety knee

Stays where it is: once, after the 4-stream sum, before `output_gain`.

- A knee per stream would not bound the sum (4 × +6 dBFS) and would colour each stream below the output ceiling.
- With four tanks the pre-knee peak rises at most by what four wet buses add; the knee's asymptote is unchanged. CL1 read pre-knee 3.93 → 1.965 (SUMMARY-2.4:86). **V**
- The R96 finite guard: keep the sum-level guard (`:4097-4107`) unchanged, and add a guard on each stream term before tank A and on `tapBuf` before tank B, both only while that tank's gate is on. With every reverb off the arithmetic, and the `wetScrubs` counts the FZ row reads (`engine_gates_23.h:892, 923`), are then today's. **A**

### A · Pitfalls

1. **Run flags past a dirty check** (memory `pattern_run_flag_past_dirty_check_latches`). `gateA`, `gateB`, `revTail`, `preHold` must be derived at every tick, unconditionally. The cutoff dirty check (`Engine.cpp:508`) must not grow to cover them.
2. **A unit that is not called cannot idle** → stale tail on re-entry. Call all 8 every piece (Q5).
3. **NaN into a tank is permanent** until a gate off → on cycle; the module's `SmoothedValue`s also keep a NaN (memory `critical_smoothedvalue_reset_does_not_clear_a_nan`). Guard the inputs; keep atomics finite (they come from clamped latches and `matrix.apply`).
4. **Class V by position** (above): the silent failure is "route does nothing".
5. **`−0 + 0`**: the stream-skip rule exists so a `+0.0f` term never joins the sum (`Engine.cpp:3945-3948`). `revTail` keeps a silent stream in the sum only while its tank was recently on, so reverb-0 hashes are safe; do not widen it.
6. **Hard controls that cannot fire** (memory `pattern_click_kappa_masked_by_level_change`): CK7 is on the return probe for that reason.
7. **Tank level under a sine depends on LFO phase** (memory `pattern_click_gate_confounds_in_a_reverb`): static references must share the tank history.
8. **Mono tank input**: at Mix 100 % in Pre the tape's stereo image is replaced by the tank's. Same as the audible wet today (`ReverbUnit.h:260`).
9. **Tail-driven erasure in Pre** (Q2 item 3): hands-on row needed.
10. **Solo toggling restarts tanks cold** (a `clearTank` per un-mute). At 192 k that is ≈ 1.3 MB per tank.
11. **Gate scope** (memory `pattern_gate_inherits_the_review_findings_scope`): IN1 exists so a shared atomic or ramp left global by mistake is caught on every parameter, not on the one someone looked at.
12. `troubleshooting/`: the reverb hits are `juce::dsp::Reverb` API notes (FlutterVerb, LushVerb); nothing applies to `ReverbUnit`.

### A · Decisions for the plan

1. **Pre = a second, loop-side tank per stream (tank B) fed by the tap; tank A stays on the audible term in both modes.** Recommended over a renormalised single tank. Cost: one extra tank per stream in Pre.
2. **`sN_fb_on` folds into the head's run target** (per-stream Freeze), not into the Amount ramp.
3. **Pre ↔ Post = tank B's `enabled` atomic**; the module's 50 ms active-gain ramp is the crossfade. No engine crossfade, no flush.
4. **Mute / solo / disable fades that stream's reverb tail in 50 ms and idles its tanks; un-mute restarts cold.** Today the tail rings out. **USER sign-off.**
5. **Reset (slot or All) flushes a Pre stream's loop tank** via a 60 ms hold on gate B. The audible tail is untouched. Without it a ringing tail rewrites the fresh copy. **USER sign-off** (small, but it defines what Reset means in Pre).
6. **Pre can hold the loop at the AGC ceiling** (bounded at 0.7, not decaying), and after note-off the head keeps writing for the tail's length. Recommended: ship as is, with the `1e-5` run threshold unchanged, an INFO row for writes-after-release and a hands-on row. Alternative if the hands-on dislikes it: a −60 dBFS run threshold for Pre streams. **USER sign-off at hands-on.**
7. **`wetKnee` stays global, after the stream sum.** Per-stream finite guards are added only while a tank's gate is on; the sum-level guard stays.
8. **FL8 stays Post-only**; in Pre the gated claim is the write bound (FL1p, FL1sp), with FL8p as INFO.
9. **Destination table**: stride 28 → 36, `NumVoiceFields = 28`, `isVoiceClass` bounded by it. Correct CONTEXT-R1's "26 → 34".
10. **No `reverb-unit` change.** 8 instances on shared size / damp / mix atomics per stream, separate `enabled` atomics.
11. **Tank gates are part of the CPU plan**, not an optimisation: `--cpu4` W max 10.41 % leaves 1.6 points. Slice Q4 should measure 4 tanks (Post, all streams enabled) and 8 tanks (C25m, Pre ×4) with these gates in.

**What exists today for Mix 0 (V).**
- The engine has no skip of its own; it always calls `reverb.process` (`Engine.cpp:4111-4115`). `revMixWasOn` is test-hook-only (`:1096-1102`).
- The module decides: `wantActive = enabled ≥ 0.5 && mix > 0.001` (`ReverbUnit.h:183-184`). Not running → returns before touching the buffer = bitwise bypass (`:201-202`). This is what keeps reverb-0 renders byte-identical.
- Tail at Mix → 0: `activeGain` ramps to 0 over 50 ms (`RAMP_SECONDS`, `:58, 204, 247`), then `running = false` on the first later call where the gain has landed (`:194-199`). Next activation: `clearTank()` + smoother snap (`:189-193, 208-218`), LFO phases reset to 0 (`:411`). Gate: RV2 ("gone within 60 ms, then bitwise the mix-0 render") and RV3 (cold restart), `engine_gates_24b.h:417-455`.
- The run flag cannot latch: it is re-derived from the atomics on every `process` call, ahead of any early return, and `prepare()` zeroes the smoother explicitly after `reset (sr, ramp)` (`:162-164`), which closes the "`SmoothedValue::reset` keeps the old target" trap from the memory note.
- A routed Mix that dips ≤ 0.1 % restarts a cold tank (R54 INFO, `Engine.cpp:3929`). This carries per stream.

**Skip design for per-stream tanks (recommendation).**
1. **Never skip the call site.** Call `tank[s].process` every piece for all four streams. The fade-out only advances inside `process` (`getNextValue` per sample). A call-site skip during the 50 ms fade freezes `activeGain` mid-ramp with `running == true`; the next call resumes a stale tail with no clear. That is exactly the latch in `pattern_run_flag_past_dirty_check_latches.md`. Idle cost of calling is 0.018 % per tank.
2. **Gate through the module's `enabled` atomic, one per stream, written at the grid tick** beside size / damp / mix (`writeReverbAtomics`, `Engine.cpp:3926-3941`): `enabled_s = streamLive_s || tailPk_s > 1e-5f`.
   - `streamLive_s` = `gp.s[s].enable && audibleTarget (s, anySolo)` or `! audibleRamp[s].settledAt (0)` (the terms the head run flag already uses, `Engine.cpp:3857` and `:3950-3951`).
   - `tailPk_s` = held peak of the tank's own output buffer from the previous cell with the existing 50 ms release (`pkRel`, the `returnPk` pattern at `Engine.cpp:3845-3853`). 1e-5 = −100 dBFS, the head's constant.
   - Derive it unconditionally every tick from buffer contents. Do not place it behind any "params unchanged" early-out, and do not compute it from a smoother's `isSmoothing()`.
   - When the module is not running the buffer is the dry stream signal, which is 0 for an inaudible stream, so `tailPk` decays and cannot hold itself up. Bounded decay: RT60 ≤ 12 s (`rt60ForSize`, `ReverbUnit.h:321`), shimmer fixed at 0, `ScopedNoDenormals` in `processBlock` (`Source/PluginProcessor.cpp:348`).
3. **Do NOT gate on input energy for an audible stream.** Today's tank free-runs from `prepare` while Mix > 0; its four LFOs' phase is absolute time. An input-silence idle would restart the LFOs at the next note and break (a) bit-identity of every single-stream reverb render, (b) the PR parity rows, which rely on "a silent pre-roll … the reverb's LFOs follow the parent's absolute-time history" (`SUMMARY-2.4.md:70`). Cost of not doing it: an enabled, silent stream keeps its tank at 0.33 %, same as today.
4. **Mixer term.** A stream's term enters the sum iff `audible[s] || tankLive_s`. With Mix 0 `tankLive` is always false, so the 2.1 skip statements (never add `+0.0f`, stream 0 always formed; `Engine.cpp:3946-3951, 4052-4091`) are untouched.
5. **Pre mode.** Stale state cannot reach the tap: every false → true edge clears the tank inside the module. While a tank is idle or fading, its buffer is (or converges to) the dry signal, so the tap sees the dry tap signal = the Post / no-reverb behaviour, never old tail. If Pre feeds the tank from the tap (question 1, other slice), the tap already carries `audibleRamp` (`Engine.cpp:3733-3737`), so an inaudible stream feeds zeros. In Pre the tank output re-enters the tape through λ ≤ 0.95 and the chain AGC, so `tailPk` still decays once voices stop; add an FA-style branch-counter gate (below) rather than trusting this by ear.
6. **Gate for the skip (not by listening).** Hook counter `tankRunCells[s]`: non-zero while the stream sounds; zero 2048 samples after `tailPk` falls; then a re-entry tail bit-compared to a never-ran control (the DX probe recipe in the memory note). Negative control: a hook that makes `enabled_s` sticky → counter never returns to 0 → row FAILS.

**Feedback chains at Amount 0 / toggle Off.**
- Today all four chains process every piece regardless of feedback, enable or audibility (`Engine.cpp:4037-4038, 3722-3784`); only the *heads* park: `parkedNow = fbEff().settledAt (0) || run.settledAt (0)` (`:3873-3876`), and a parked head writes nothing (`:3895-3900`; FL4: 0 writes, tapes bitwise pristine).
- **Toggle Off = λ target 0 through the same 20 ms ramp**: `fbRamp[s].setTarget (on ? amount : 0)`. Then Off ≡ Amount 0 for the tape by construction (same park path), which is the CONTEXT constraint. The routed arm must be forced too: `Engine.cpp:3822-3829` takes the matrix value when `Feedback` is routed, so Off has to zero `k` after `matrix.apply`, or a route writes to tape with the toggle Off.
- **Do not add a chain skip in R1.** It saves well under the 0.87 % bypass figure, and it changes what a chain holds when feedback rises from 0 (cold instead of warm filters / AGC / diffuser), which alters CK2, FL5 and FA4 stimuli for no budget reason.

**Mitigations if a later measurement disagrees, cheapest first.**
1. Audibility + tail gating as designed above (engine-only, no module edit). Recommended regardless. The tail semantic (ring out, then idle) overlaps question 5 → **user sign-off on the semantic**, not on the CPU.
2. Content lever in the D64 script: write Mix 0 on streams the preset does not enable (33 + 24 presets). Zero DSP cost; but enabling a stream in the UI then starts dry. **User sign-off** (changes "copy to all four").
3. Shimmer path removal / fixed-size tank variant: a module fork or 1.3.0 option. Not needed; **user sign-off** if ever proposed.
4. D21 engine levers (Hermite gating, SIMD, FX-skip): unchanged order, not needed.

**(iii) Reverb: "four identical Post tanks, summed" vs "one tank on the sum".**

- **`jlimit (−2, 2, …)` on every tank write** (`:282`). Silent below |2|, a hard clip above.

### B · Pitfalls

1. **Call-site skip of a tank = the documented latch.** The 50 ms fade only advances inside `process`. Always call; gate with the `enabled` atomic. (**V** `ReverbUnit.h:183-204`.)
2. **Input-energy idle on an audible stream breaks bit-identity and PR parity** (LFO phase is absolute time today). Gate on audibility + tail only. (**V** `ReverbUnit.h:411`, `SUMMARY-2.4.md:70`.)
3. **`+0.0f` terms.** Adding an idle stream's term turns −0 into +0 and breaks the 2.1 hashes. Keep the `have` logic and stream 0's always-formed term. (**V** `Engine.cpp:3946-3948, 4052-4091`.)
4. **FMA contraction** changes with statement shape; the engine is not built `-ffp-contract=off`. (**V** `CMakeLists.txt:249-254`.)
5. **The tank's ±2 write clip** makes four tanks ≠ one tank at stress levels (−1 dB null). CL1 / FL1 printed values move; thresholds hold. Do not chase it as a regression. (**V** bench.)
6. **Toggle Off must also beat a routed Feedback**, or a mod route writes to tape with the stream switched off. (**V** `Engine.cpp:3822-3829`.)
7. **`updateChainCutoffs` dirty check** is one pair today; per stream it needs four cached pairs, otherwise three chains never get their coefficients. (**V** `Engine.cpp:508-513`.)
8. **`clearTank` on the audio thread ×4** in one cell at a preset load: 4 × 393 KB of `std::fill`. No allocation (FA4 stays 0), but add the four-tank re-enable to FA4's script. (**V** `ReverbUnit.h:401-413`.)
9. **`--cpu4` W is "as authored", not 16 voices** (`VERIFICATION.md:123`). The four-tank worst case is C25m only; `c25Extra` (`engine_gates_24.h:1281-1285`) must set all four streams' Mix / Size / feedback or it under-reads by 0.99 points.
10. **Speed witness drift.** Calib moved 8 % within one run today (load average 3.8, other sessions active). Run `--cpu4` / `--cpu26` alone, in the background (≈ 7.5 min; 600 s watchdog), and compare by the session factor, not raw points.
11. **Param-dump console** must never run without `OGI_NO_FACTORY_WRITE`; the checkpoint script already checks `~/Library/…/Factory` before and after — keep that check at every B-step.
12. **FP0's coverage checks read global IDs by name** with `val (s, "reverb_mix", 0)` defaults; after the port a missing key silently reads the default 0 and fails "Pads" / "Drones" for the wrong reason. Port to `s1_…` (or max over enabled streams) in the same commit as the bank.
13. **A gate written against "stream 1" only** leaves streams 2–4 unproven: every new per-stream row needs an arm on s ≥ 2 (index and atomic wiring are per stream).

### B · Decisions for the plan

1. **Four `ReverbUnit` 1.2.0 instances, no module edit, no cheaper tank.** Measured +0.99 points worst case (C25m ≈ 29.9 / 40), typical max ≈ 9.6 / 12 with no skip. Recommend as is.
2. **Tank gating = per-stream `enabled` atomic written at the tick: `streamLive || tailPk > 1e-5`; `process` always called.** Recommend. **USER sign-off** on the audible consequence: a muted / disabled stream's tail rings out, then the tank idles and restarts cold (joint with question 5).
3. **No input-energy idle while a stream is audible.** Recommend; costs 0.33 % per enabled silent stream, same as today, and keeps single-stream renders bitwise.
4. **No chain skip.** Four chains already run; Off = λ target 0 on the existing ramp, forced after the matrix. Recommend.
5. **Knee stays on the stream sum after the four tanks** (required for single-stream bit-identity). Recommend; question 5's owner to confirm.
6. **Render goldens: zero re-pins.** `renders-2.1 / 2.2 / 2.4` stay as the regression anchors and are checked at every step (B0–B4).
7. **Baseline step B0 before any source edit**, including a new `--dump-preset-renders` mode run on the unmodified tree. Recommend as task 1 of the plan.
8. **Land DSP (B1) before the parameter surface (B2) before the bank (B3).** Recommend; each step has a bitwise or −110 dB comparison against B0.
9. **Multi-stream preset comparison under a tolerance row** (≤ −110 dB, |g − 1| ≤ 1e-5, `differ > 0` for multi-stream and `== 0` for single-stream), with the three controls listed. No user decision.

11. **Stress-level behaviour change accepted:** above the tank's ±2 internal clip, per-stream tanks sound cleaner than one summed tank. Inform the **USER** (one line in CHANGELOG / hands-on row at FL1-like settings); no action.
12. **`c25Extra`, C25m, `--cpu4` ported to per-stream IDs and re-run alone in the background**; add an INFO row "C25m, four tanks Pre" once question 1 fixes the Pre topology (a second tank per stream would add ≈ 1.3 points more).

### D · Pitfalls

1. **Per-stream ≠ per-voice.** The 32 new destinations sit inside the stream blocks but are class G. Any code that treats "stream destination" as "voice class" (`GiDest::isVoiceClass`, `GiVoiceMod.h:322`, `Engine.cpp:1964, 2976, 3417`, page `isGlobalDest`, `ui_gates_33.h:119-129`, `ui_frontend_check.js:911`) silently puts them on the per-voice path or drops the mono badge. `GiModMap::streamFieldFromRaw` (`GiModMatrix.h:112+`) switches on the field and must not receive fields ≥ 28 unhandled.
2. **Preset JSON is normalised.** Growing the choice list re-maps every stored `modM_dest`, including indices that did not move. Pre-R1 user presets on the dev machine are all wrong and cannot be detected.
3. **`extract_spec.py` asserts "only `s1_enable` defaults On"** (:316) and 19 setSkewForCentre rows (:308). A default-On `sN_fb_enable` fails the extractor until both are edited.
4. **`data-dest` is literal in the page** (133 attributes). The page learns destination names from it (`index.html:3039-3060`); a stale number mislabels a route rather than failing loudly. DM2 in `ui_binding_probe.js` covers "every destination 1…N has a cell" — keep it and move its literal at :333.
5. **Forked file.** `kGiModDests` and the global-id binds live in `Source/dsp/forked/ControlGrid.h`; `tests/fork-drift-check.sh` compares against FORK-MANIFEST local hashes.
6. **Auto-clear and existing gates.** Rows that pump the message loop after a take (CM1) will find the arm Off; OS1's meaning shrinks to the engine level. Audit before adding AC rows, or the first failure looks like a regression.
7. **Auto-clear from the wrong thread.** `pollRecorder()` also runs on `RecPollThread` when there is no MessageManager. The parameter write must be Timer-path only.
8. **Row 9 and row 11 (inert / stale On).** If only commit and discard clear the arm, an arm made with no sidechain bus (the F1 situation in the Standalone) stays On and the lane Record button stays dead — the exact symptom D66 is meant to remove.
9. **Preset apply never touches `rec_arm`.** Do not "fix" a stale On by removing it from the excluded set: the reset-then-apply edge would arm a capture (`PluginProcessor.cpp:111-113`).
10. **FP2 loudness spread** (≤ 9 dB) and `--cpu4` (≤ 12 %) are the two gates the showcase re-voicing can break; re-trim with `sN_level`, never `output_gain`.
11. **Wildcard keys and `validateSpec`.** If expansion runs in `makeFactoryPresets` only, `validateSpec` reports "unknown s*_reverb_mix" (jassert in debug, and FP0 fails). Expand in both, or expand once inside `factoryPresetSpecs()`.
12. **CHANGELOG heading.** Keep `## [1.0.0] - <date>` as the only version heading (release-notes extractor; memory `pattern_changelog_heading_must_be_bracket_form…`).
