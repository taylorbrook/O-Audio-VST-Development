## Stage 3 decisions (carried from `v1-ui.yaml` open_questions)

1. **Summed curve with L / R / M / S bands.** The mockup drew one ink line as the plain dB sum of all bands, which is not a true response once placements are mixed. The page composes the bands as 2x2 matrices and draws ONE diagonal term, chosen by the selected band's placement (Stereo and Left show LL, Right RR, Mid MM, Side SS; nothing selected shows LL). With Stereo-only bands all four coincide. Alternatives: per-placement curves at once, or always LL. One function decides it: `sumView()` in the page. Needs the user's call
2. **Header padding.** Header controls sit 6 px from the top edge (thin 40 px header by request). The generic 15 px edge-padding rule would cost 16 px of display height. Kept at 6 px
3. **Typed entry versus double-click reset.** A single click on a strip field opens typed entry after the double-click window (240 ms, `ENTRY_CLICK_MS`) so that double-click can reset. Alternative: open at once and move reset to Alt-click. Kept as in the mockup
4. **Empty chip.** Clicking an empty chip enables that band index at its STORED values (so a deleted band comes back as it was). A click on the plot instead takes the next free band and makes it a Bell, Stereo, not bypassed, default Q, at the clicked frequency and gain; slope is left as stored (inert for a bell). Confirm both
5. **Dragonfly engraving.** The image library has no dragonfly plate. Production needs a real engraving (PNG, transparent ground, sepia hairlines, no fills). Until then the plate box is empty. The mockup's inline procedural SVG was a design-time sketch and was not carried forward
6. **Stereo glyph.** `LR` (not `S` / `St`), so that `S` can mean Side. Kept

Also open from `stages/0-ideation/CONTEXT.md` "Needs the user's call before Stage 1" and visible in this UI: tilt convention (4), Q 1.0 = Butterworth on cuts and shelves (5), listen filter definition (8).

## Deviations from the generator's defaults, and why

- **Value fields, not knobs, for band frequency / gain / Q.** The agent contract says every continuous parameter is a Family A knob. The approved design edits the 72 band sliders on the curve and in three strip fields, so those are fields with the same lifecycle (pointer capture, `pointerup` / `pointercancel` / `lostpointercapture`, arrow keys, wheel gesture, double-click reset, `aria-valuetext`). The two global sliders are Family A knobs with `bindKnob`
- **Reflowing, free-aspect resizable frame** instead of Rule 4's fixed frame or locked-aspect whole-page scale: user decision, recorded in the YAML
- **No magnitude model in the page.** The mockup evaluated analog prototypes. The page holds only the two closed-form section responses and draws no curve until `eqSections` arrives (Phase 3.2). Nodes, chips and the strip work without it. In Phase 3.1 the display therefore shows the grid and nodes but no curves; that is expected
- **No fake spectrum.** The analyzer wash draws only from `analyzerPre` / `analyzerPost` (Phase 3.3)
- **Chip and disabled-control tooltips are static.** The mockup composed them in JS ("Band 3: Bell, 2.8 kHz"); tooltip copy may only come from `TIP_BINDINGS`, so `#chips` carries one tip
- **Hover readout on the canvas** shows numbers, units and the placement letter only, so it needs no translation
- **`displayRange` and `sampleRate`** additions, noted in section 2
