### Four corrections the executor must not re-derive

**C-1 · The non-ASCII gate in the task brief is not executable as written, and its obvious
repair is a false pass.** `grep -P` does not exist in macOS BSD grep. The byte-class repair
`LC_ALL=C grep -rl $'[\x80-\xff]'` returns **empty on a file full of French accents** — a
silent false pass, proven by running it against `i18n.js` as a control.

**C-2 · O-Chorus's C++ already contains non-ASCII, so a blanket non-ASCII grep can never be
zero.** Measured across `Source/**/*.{h,cpp}`: U+2014 x5, U+20E3 x6, U+2192 x4, U+2212 x1,
U+2500 x41, U+26A0 x2, U+FE0F x8 — box-drawing and typographic characters in comments. The
gate must scope to **Han**, not to non-ASCII. The working gate, with its control, is
`HAN_GATE` below.

**C-3 · `--emit <Name>` does NOT scope the walk.** In `i18n-zh-backtranslate.js`,
`val('--emit')` is written into the manifest's `target` field and nothing else; `collect()`
scopes on `val('--plugin')`. Today the `r.zh` filter makes them look equivalent because only
O-Chorus will carry zh — but that is accidental. **Always pass `--plugin O-Chorus` alongside
`--emit`/`--ingest`.**

**C-4 · `plugins/O-Chorus/tests/ui_tip_render_check.js:386` hard-codes
`for (const lang of ['en','fr','en'])`, and `:382` hard-codes `{ en: {}, fr: {} }`.** Stage 0
extended the two repo-level gates and did not touch per-plugin test files. Re-run unchanged,
this gate passes **vacuously** for Chinese — research P-5's exact failure mode. It is also the
**only** gate that measures a tooltip against the 125 px frame; `check-ui-labels` measures
captions, not the hover surface. It is therefore in scope as **ZH2-08**.

per C-4. Justification: it is not a difficulty judgement, it closes a proven vacuity hole in a
gate the checklist already relies on ("back-translation read, tooltips fit the 125 px frame"),
and Stage 3's own brief already requires exactly this treatment for the two other per-plugin
gates. Adding it here means Stage 3 inherits the pattern instead of rediscovering it.

**No tracer task, and the reason is architectural, not a difficulty call.** A partial fill is
not a legal intermediate state: the instant `LANGUAGES` gains `'zh-Hans'`, `check-i18n`
assertion `[1]` requires a `zh-Hans` string on **every** key, and `check-ui-labels` assertion 2
(`MIN_LANG_DIFF_FRACTION = 0.25`, `:118`) fails any pass where under a quarter of rendered
labels changed. A 2-entry spine therefore cannot reach a green gate, so the thin slice would
have to be verified by something weaker than the real gates — which is the opposite of what a
tracer is for. The 28-entry fill is the smallest slice that any gate can honestly measure.
