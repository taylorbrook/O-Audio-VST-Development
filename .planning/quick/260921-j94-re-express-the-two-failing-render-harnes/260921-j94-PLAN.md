Two things this table settles. First, `delta` is a DIFFERENCE of two independently measured
best-of-3 timings, so the ratio of deltas amplifies noise: run 34808363835's BL baseline (13.50)
reads LOWER than its 2x baseline (14.77) for work that is nearly identical. The total ratio spans
1.04-1.30 where the delta ratio spans 1.15-1.97 on the same three runs. Second — and this is the
load-bearing conclusion — **a ratio alone does not fix this row.** Any K that admits 1.97 cannot
detect a 90 % regression; any K tight enough to detect one fails the contended reading of 3.9.
The memory note's prescription is therefore two-part: ratio **and** a duty-cycle witness that
makes the gate skip rather than fail on a contended machine.
