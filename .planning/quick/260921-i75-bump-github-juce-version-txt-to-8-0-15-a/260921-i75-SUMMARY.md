# Quick Task 260921-i75: Bump the JUCE pin to 8.0.15 Summary

The JUCE pin moved 8.0.14 → 8.0.15 and the two vendored override files were re-derived from the
8.0.15 tag rather than carried forward — which is the entire point, because upstream changed one of
the two override targets between those releases and a naive bump would have silently reverted that
fix in every CI binary.

## What Happened

Upstream's 8.0.15 `getPosition()` fix is now **in** the vendored wrapper instead of being overwritten
by 8.0.14 bytes. The regeneration produced exactly the delta bounded in advance at planning time
(P6): the `.h` byte-unchanged, the `.cpp` one insertion / one deletion.

```diff
-        info.setTimeInSamples (jmax ((Steinberg::int64) 0, processContext.projectTimeSamples));
+        info.setTimeInSamples (processContext.projectTimeSamples);
```

This was the task's one High-severity risk and it discharged cleanly. The generator built in
260921-hno did its job on first real use, with no hand-editing of anything under `vendored/`.
